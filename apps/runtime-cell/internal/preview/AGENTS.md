# apps/runtime-cell/internal/preview

The cell's side of preview URLs ([`docs/runtime/preview-urls.md`](../../../../docs/runtime/preview-urls.md) §4): the routes the hub declared (`preview.route`, `preview.unroute`), the WebSocket the cell dials to the preview edge (`preview.tunnel`) and the HTTP/2 server on it, which passes a stream to its claim's observer only while a live route names the stream's preview id. It runs in the cell process; the agent (`internal/agent/preview.go`) hands it each route with its upstream.

## Owns
- Routes: `routes.go` `Table` (`Set`, `Remove`, `RemoveClaim`, `Len`), `Route`, `Target`, `ValidID`, `ErrOtherClaim`; removing a route cancels its open streams.
- The tunnel: `tunnel.go` `Service` (`New`, `Tunnel`, `Route`, `Unroute`, `ReleaseClaim`, `Connected`, `CloseIdle`, `Close`), `AuthScheme`, `DefaultIdleClose`.
- Serving a stream: `handler.go` (path and body checks, per-preview limits `DefaultMaxStreams`, `DefaultMaxRequestBody`, `DefaultRequestTimeout`, byte and stream counters), `ObserverUpstream` (a reverse proxy to the observer's `/v1/preview` over the agent's mTLS transport).

## Does not own
- Which claims may preview and when a route is declared: `internal/agent/preview.go` and the hub.
- Proxying into the app: `internal/observer` `PreviewHandler`.
- The edge and the preview ids: `apps/runtime` (preview edge), `libs/runtime/contracts/preview.ts`.

## Rules and limits
- It never imports the agent, the hub client, a driver or the data plane, and the observer never imports it ([`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) "Package boundaries").
- A stream reaches an app only through a live route of its claim, past its `Until`, and only its http expose entry; another claim cannot take a routed id. Preview traffic never becomes probe evidence.
- The edge's `Kodus-Preview-Id` and `Kodus-Preview-Session` headers never reach the app. Refusals carry `observer.PreviewRefusedHeader` (`no_route`, `busy`, `too_large`, `upstream`).
- TLS 1.3 at least, with the cell's client certificate and the edge's roots; the ticket rides in its own `Authorization` scheme. The tunnel closes `DefaultIdleClose` after its last route.
- The per-preview limits, the auth scheme and the header names are literals here, not constants generated from `libs/runtime/contracts/preview.ts`; only the id pattern comes from `protocol.PreviewIDPattern`.

## Where new code goes
- A new preview limit or header: in `libs/runtime/contracts/preview.ts` first, then here, with a case in `preview_test.go`.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/preview` (both ends of the WebSocket in process, `clock.Fake`).

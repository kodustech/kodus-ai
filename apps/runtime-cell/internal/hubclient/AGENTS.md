# apps/runtime-cell/internal/hubclient

The cell's one connection to the runtime-hub: an outbound WebSocket with mTLS, no compression, 1 MiB frames, and the protocol's ack/nack rule (every request answered within `AckTimeout`, 5 s). A port under `internal/agent` ([package rules](../../AGENTS.md), "Package boundaries"); it runs in the cell process, and the observer (`cmd/observer`) dials with it too.

## Owns
- `conn.go` `Dial`: the TLS config must carry the cell's client certificate and the hub CA; nothing else is trusted.
- `Conn.Serve`: the read loop. Frames the decoder refuses never reach the handler; a frame addressed to another cell, or a binary frame, is refused and counted (`RefusedFrames`).
- Answering: a `Handler`'s nil error is an ack, a `*NackError` a nack as is, any other error a nack `internal`. `Later` runs the answer off the read loop under a context that ends before the hub's ack budget.
- Sending: `Conn.Send`, `Request` and `RequestAck` (wait for ack or nack, `ErrAckTimeout`); `Sender` is what the agent needs of a connection.
- Nack fields clipped to the protocol's limits on a rune boundary.

## Does not own
- What each frame means, and redelivery of claim frames until acked: `internal/agent` (`handle.go`) and `internal/agent/outbox`.
- The frame format and its strict decoder: `internal/protocol`.
- The client certificate and its renewal: `internal/enrollment`.

## Rules and limits
- A `Handler` runs on the read loop and must return at once; work that needs I/O before the answer goes through `Later` ([package rules](../../AGENTS.md), "Package boundaries").
- A duplicate answer never blocks delivery.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/hubclient` (`testpki`, an in-process WebSocket server). `internal/testing/hubstub` and `test/integration` drive it end to end.

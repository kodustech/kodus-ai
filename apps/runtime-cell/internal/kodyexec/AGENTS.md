# apps/runtime-cell/internal/kodyexec

The command runner behind every driver's exec: a command under a deadline, its whole process group killed at the end, its output capped and its secrets masked. On the cell side the agent, the drivers, `dockercli` and `transport` use its `Request`, `Result` and `RunArgv`. Its output is evidence, never a verdict (ADR-053): the PR can be root in the VM and forge anything printed here.

## Owns
- `exec.go` `Run` (a shell line) and `RunArgv`: the deadline, `TimeoutExitCode` (124), `KillGrace`, output capped at `MaxStream` per stream.
- `Request` (command, cwd, env, timeout, `Repository` for a claim of several repositories), `Limits` (per-command rlimits, S-09) and `Request.ShellLine`.
- `BecomeSubreaper`: orphans reparent to the process, so a double fork cannot outlive the timeout; `kill_linux.go` kills the group.
- `mask.go` `MaskSecrets`, `SecretVariants` (through `redact.Variants`), `Redaction`.

## Does not own
- Where a command runs (docker exec, the guest agent): `internal/transport`, the drivers, `internal/guest`.
- The last redaction of what leaves the cell: `internal/redact` and the agent.

## Rules and limits
- A runaway command always ends with a result, even with a setsid child or a background job holding its output.
- Only a process that exists for one command calls `BecomeSubreaper`; the cell agent must not.
- `Limits` are a second lock under the machine's own pids, memory and disk limits; zero means unset.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/kodyexec` (real child processes; the setsid case needs Linux).

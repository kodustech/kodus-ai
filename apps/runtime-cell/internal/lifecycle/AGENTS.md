# apps/runtime-cell/internal/lifecycle

Who owns a process's long-lived work: its servers, its loops and a driver's background work. Every goroutine it starts has an owner that waits for it, and every wait has a deadline. A leaf ([package rules](../../AGENTS.md), "Package boundaries"): every `cmd/*` that serves runs in it, and `internal/drivers/dockergvisor` runs its background work in its own `Group`.

## Owns
- `lifecycle.go` `Group`: `Go` (a named part; a failing part ends the group), `Stop` (end every part and wait, `ErrGaveUp` naming what still runs at the deadline), `Drain` (let running work finish, then end its context at the deadline).
- `Run`: start parts, stop on SIGTERM or SIGINT or a failed start, then run the `after` funcs (what the parts used, closed last). `Within` is its deadline.
- `ServeHTTP`: an `http.Server` as a part that finishes the request in flight.
- `WaitWithin`; `Usage` and `ExitCode` (a usage error exits 2).

## Rules and limits
- The rules that only `main` exits and that every goroutine has an owner with a deadline: [package rules](../../AGENTS.md), "Package boundaries". This package is how they are kept.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/lifecycle` (pure, signals sent to a test context).

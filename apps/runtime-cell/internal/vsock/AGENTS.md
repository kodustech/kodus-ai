# apps/runtime-cell/internal/vsock

The byte stream between the cell and the guest agent in a Firecracker microVM ([`docs/runtime/isolation-architecture.md`](../../../../docs/runtime/isolation-architecture.md) C4). Two sides: `Dial` is the host's, used by the Firecracker backend; `Listen` and `ListenNet` (Linux) are the guest's, used by `internal/guestinit` and `cmd/kody-guest`.

## Owns
- The host side: `vsock.go` `Dial` (connect to the VM's vsock unix socket, write `CONNECT <port>`, read `OK <host port>`), `ErrNotListening`, `Addr`, `GuestPort` (1024), `GuestCID` (3).
- The guest side: `listen_linux.go` `Listener` (`Listen`, `Accept`, `AcceptFrom` with the peer's CID), `ListenNet` (a `net.Listener` for the observer's HTTPS server); `listen_other.go` refuses off Linux.

## Does not own
- The frames on the stream (length-prefixed protocol frames, the same as over `docker exec -i` on gVisor): `internal/guest`.
- The VM's vsock device and its unix socket path: `internal/isolation/firecracker`.

## Rules and limits
- An acknowledgement that is not `OK` is refused; bytes the guest sends right after it reach the caller.
- Inside a machine: it imports nothing of the control side ([`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) "Package boundaries").

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/vsock` (the host handshake against a fake unix socket, anywhere).
- `loopback_linux_test.go` on a real kernel: `KODY_E2E_VSOCK_LOOPBACK=1` with the `vsock_loopback` module.

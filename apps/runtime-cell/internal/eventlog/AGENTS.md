# apps/runtime-cell/internal/eventlog

The bounded, sequenced log a data-plane component keeps for the cell agent to long-poll: the egress proxy's denials and connections, and the egress proxy's and injector's alerts. Every entry gets the next sequence number, the oldest fall off, and a reader asks for anything after the last number it saw. A leaf, used on both sides of the control port.

## Owns
- `eventlog.go` `New`, `Log.Add` (the entry built with its sequence number), `Log.After` (long poll, at most `MaxWait`), `Log.Handler` (`GET ?after=&wait=` as a JSON array).
- The negotiated poll: with `X-Kodus-Eventlog-Version`, the handler answers the log's epoch, oldest and latest sequence and a continuity `Reason`, and refuses a malformed cursor or header.
- `continuity.go` `Reason`, `FeedError`, `HistoryLost` (a producer restart, a retention gap or an invalid cursor: retrying with a fresh cursor cannot repair it), `FailureReason`.

## Does not own
- What an entry is: `internal/egress` (`Event`, `Alert`), `internal/injector`.
- Reading the feeds and what a lost history means for a claim: `internal/agent` (`alerts.go`, `source_health.go`).

## Rules and limits
- A `Reason` names the feed's health only, never a claim's security verdict, and a `FeedError` carries no upstream response or URL.
- The epoch is random per process: a restarted producer is detected, not silently resumed.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/eventlog` (pure, `httptest`).

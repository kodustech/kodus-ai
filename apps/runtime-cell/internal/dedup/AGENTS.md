# apps/runtime-cell/internal/dedup

At-most-once execution of a claim's steps: the cell keeps every step it has seen, by `stepId`, for an hour, so the hub's retry of a step it already ran is acked as a duplicate and not run again. A pure package with its own lock, used by `internal/agent` when it admits `exec` and probe frames.

## Owns
- `dedup.go` `Store.Admit`: records the step and answers a `Verdict`: `New` (run it), `Duplicate` (same claim, step id and command hash), `Conflict` (the step id was seen with another command or claim: the frame gets a nack, S-30).
- `Store.AdmitIf`: checks existing entries first and records new work only when `reserve` accepts it, else `Refused` and nothing is recorded.
- `DefaultTTL` (one hour) and the expiry sweep, at most once a minute.

## Does not own
- What a step's canonical command bytes are (command and cwd, or the probe), and the ack or nack sent: `internal/agent`.

## Rules and limits
- Step ids are global (hub UUIDs), not per claim: one seen on another claim is a `Conflict`.
- Call `Admit` last, after every other admission check: a step recorded and then refused would be acked as a `Duplicate` on retry and never run.
- `reserve` runs under the store's lock: it must not block, do I/O or call the store.
- Entries live in memory: a restarted cell forgets them.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/dedup` (pure, a fake `now`).

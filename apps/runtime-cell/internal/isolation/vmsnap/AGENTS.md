# apps/runtime-cell/internal/isolation/vmsnap

The rules and the store of microVM snapshots (`docs/runtime/isolation-p3-snapshots.md`): which machine may become a snapshot and for what, what a snapshot is on disk, whether this host can restore it, and when it goes. It holds no machine and reaches no VMM: `isolation/firecracker` asks it before it snapshots and before it restores, and keeps its files in the `Store`. `cmd/cell` uses it for `cell template export` and `import` (`template.go`); the agent reads `ErrRefused`.

## Owns
- The gate: `gate.go` `Admit` over the backend's own `Facts` (never the caller's word), `KnownTaint`, `ErrRefused` / `RefusedError`.
- The format: `manifest.go` `Manifest` (schema, files and their digests, org, purpose, key, kit and host), `Manifest.Check`, `Manifest.Restorable` against `Here`, `HostFingerprint`, `ValidID`; `files_unix.go` digests of sparse files by their runs of data.
- The store: `store.go` `Store` (`Stage`, `Publish`, `Open`, `Claim` for a session's one restore, `Latest`, `RetireKey`, `RemoveClaim`, `Remove`), its bounds `Policy` (`Sweep`, `Room`, `Usage`, `OrphanGrace`).
- Moving a template between hosts: `export.go` `Export`, `Import` (a PAX tar with each file's runs and time to the nanosecond; holes stay holes, digests checked, manifest published last).

## Does not own
- Taking and restoring a snapshot (pausing the VM, the VMM's snapshot calls, the guest making a clone its own): `internal/isolation/firecracker` `snapshot.go`, `internal/guestinit`.
- Which purpose the agent asks for and when: `internal/agent` (`snapshots.go`).

## Rules and limits
- The org must be the machine's own. A machine handed secrets, credentials or canaries never becomes a snapshot of any kind; a pooled machine never does; a template comes only from a setup's machine that never ran a PR's code; a round is the claim's own and is restored only into that claim's same machine.
- A snapshot opens only for its org, only where it can be restored (same kit and host fingerprint), and only while its files are what was published (digest and time).
- Only a template leaves its host: `Export` refuses a round or a session, and checks the template as a restore would.
- The store's disk is bounded in total and per org (`Policy.MaxBytes`, `MaxOrgBytes`); `Room` refuses, before it is taken, a snapshot that would take the org or the store past them. A round whose claim the cell no longer holds goes after `OrphanGrace`.

## Where new code goes
- A new rule on what may be snapshotted: `Admit`, with its fact recorded by the backend in `Facts`.
- A new field a restore must match: `Manifest` and `Here`, with a test that a mismatch is refused; bump `Schema` when old manifests no longer read.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/isolation/vmsnap` (temporary directories, no VMM).

# apps/runtime-cell/internal/agent/evidence

The evidence of a step: what the app did while a probe ran (service logs, database effects, outbound calls) on the round's clock `[t0, t1]`, and an exec's output as an asciicast. A stateless part of the agent, in the cell process; it sees a claim only as `evidence.Claim` (id, logger, workload, redactor), built by the agent's `evidence_claim.go` `evidenceOf`.

## Owns
- `evidence.go` `Collector`: `Asked` (the step asked for a capture and this hub session accepts its field), `AttachProbe` (replaces the observer's evidence with the round's), `ExecCast` (asciicast v2 of an exec), `Upload` (one artifact PUT with its grant).
- The bounds: 256 KB of logs (newest kept) with the last 50 lines inline, 200 outbound calls, 64 KB of database rows, 512 KB of cast, 30 s per read and per upload.

## Does not own
- Reading service logs (`driver.ServiceLogReader`, the driver's `Parts.ServiceLogs`) and the egress record (`OutboundSource`, wired in `cmd/cell` from `policy/control`).
- The canary check: the runtime-worker reads every text artifact back before anyone can open it (ADR-043). The asciicast format: `internal/asciicast`.

## Rules and limits
- Every text is redacted with the claim's redactor before it is uploaded or summarized; a database cell is redacted as text or over its JSON (`redactCell`).
- An upload keeps to its grant: its content type only, within its bytes, before it expires, no redirect followed. The grant's URL carries its signature and is never logged.
- A block the cell could not read is left out ("not captured"), never sent empty. Nothing here changes a probe result the gate reads.
- A nil `ServiceLogs` or `Outbound` is a cell that cannot read them: that block is left out.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/agent/evidence`; the agent's probe and exec tests cover it end to end (`internal/agent`).

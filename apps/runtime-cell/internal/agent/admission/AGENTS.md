# apps/runtime-cell/internal/agent/admission

The cell's memory admission ledger (ADR-070): megabytes counted per claim id against what the cell may commit. A part of the agent with its own lock, in the cell process. On a driver whose claims share the cell's memory (`driver.MemoryBudget`), the agent admits a claim only when its count fits; the heartbeat reports what the ledger holds so the hub stops sending what would not fit.

## Owns
- `ledger.go` `Ledger`: `Admit` (a new claim at its build size, refused when it does not fit unless `enforce` is false), `Set` (re-count a held claim, reports over-commit), `Forget`, `Count`, `Committed`, `Allocatable`.
- Asynchronous holds: `Retain` reserves budget for work that outlives the claim's release (a bake on the claim's physical tree); its `release` is idempotent and stays counted after `Forget`. `Holds` counts them.
- Warm spares: `Spare` reserves room for a second copy of a live claim's environment (docker-gvisor's warm fork reset) only when it fits after every claim, hold and other spare; `Committed` (the heartbeat) never counts it, and `Admit`, `Set` and `Retain` evict the newest spares the moment a claim or a hold needs their room. `EndSpare` drops one that was taken, failed or ended. A spare never makes a claim refuse or wait.

## Does not own
- What a claim is counted for (build size, ready size) and when: `internal/agent` `memory.go`.
- The cell's allocatable budget: `cmd/cell` `memory.go` `allocatableMemoryMB` and the driver's `Parts.Memory`.

## Rules and limits
- Knows no driver, claim or hub: ids and megabytes only ([`apps/runtime-cell/AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md), parts with state of their own).
- A `Retain` release runs only when the driver operation returns, not when it is cancelled.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/agent/admission` (pure).

# apps/runtime-cell/internal/agent/outbox

Delivery of the cell's claim-scoped frames until the hub acks them, over whatever hub connection the cell holds at each attempt. A part of the agent, in the cell process; the agent's `deliver` and `deliverOnce` (`internal/agent/settle.go`) are its only callers.

## Owns
- `outbox.go` `Outbox.Deliver`: resend with doubling backoff (6 attempts, 1 s to 30 s by default, on a `clock.Clock`), stop at an ack, a nack, a frame the protocol refuses (`protocol.ErrRefusedToSend`), the context's end, or a frame no longer wanted (`Frame.Wanted`, `ErrUnwanted`).
- `Frame.Once`: one try, no retry, for a frame a newer one replaces (`claim.progress`).
- The delivery metrics: `cell_hub_deliveries_total` by kind and outcome, `cell_hub_delivery_retries_total`.

## Does not own
- Redaction of the bodies: the agent's `redactOutbound` before `Deliver`. The hub connection and ack timing: `internal/hubclient`.

## Rules and limits
- The hub is idempotent by `claimId` and `stepId`, so a resend of what it has is acked. Each attempt gets a new envelope id.
- Frames leave only through the agent's `deliver` and `deliverOnce` ([`apps/runtime-cell/AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md) "Untrusted input").

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/agent/outbox` (fake clock, no sleeps).

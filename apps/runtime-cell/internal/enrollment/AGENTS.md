# apps/runtime-cell/internal/enrollment

How a cell gets and keeps its short client certificate (ADR-054): a key made on the cell that never leaves it, a CSR, and the hub's answer checked and written so a crash never leaves a key without its certificate. It runs in the cell process: `cell register`, `cell renew` and the agent's renewal loop (`cmd/cell`).

## Owns
- `enroll.go` `NewKeyAndCSR`, `RequestCertificate` (the answer must be a certificate for this key, valid now, naming the cell, and chaining to the issuer), `Renew` (over the same mTLS path as `cell renew`), `HTTPSBase`.
- `files.go` `Paths`, `WriteIdentity` (key file first, then the certificate, each by temp file, sync and rename), `LoadIdentity`, `WriteHubCA`; `ErrNoIssuer` when the pinned issuer is missing.
- `renewer.go` `CertHolder` (the live certificate, swapped without dropping connections; `ClientTLS`), `RenewAt` (a third of the validity before the end), `Renewer.Run` (renew, write, swap; on failure an alarm and backoff from `MinBackoff` to `MaxBackoff`).

## Does not own
- Issuing certificates and the cell directory: the hub (`apps/runtime/src/hub`).
- The commands' flags: `cmd/cell`.

## Rules and limits
- Two CAs, two files, never mixed: the hub CA (`Paths.CA`) verifies the hub and comes from the operator, never from a hub answer; the issuer (`Paths.Issuer`) signed the cell's certificate, comes from register, and every renewal must chain to it.
- Nothing here prints key material.
- A renewal failure logs `cert.renew.failed` with `alarm`, and `cert.expired` once past the end; a certificate already due on arrival waits like a failure instead of looping on the hub.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/enrollment` (`testpki`, a test hub): crash between renames, issuer pinning, backoff and alarms.

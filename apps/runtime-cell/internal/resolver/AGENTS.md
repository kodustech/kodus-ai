# apps/runtime-cell/internal/resolver

The only DNS a claim's machines and the observer have. It answers the cell's own names (`policy.AppHost`, `policy.MirrorHost`) and the claim's exact allowlist; every other name gets NXDOMAIN without any query leaving the cell. On the data plane: it runs in `cmd/resolver`, or inside `cmd/egress` beside the proxy, which also uses it for its own lookups.

## Owns
- `resolver.go` `Server` (`ServeDNS`, `LookupA` for the proxy, `ForgetClaim`): the claim found by the query's source through `policy.Store.ForSource`, answers cached for their TTL (at most 5 minutes, 10 000 entries), a token bucket per source (50 qps, burst 200 by default), large UDP answers truncated.
- What it hands out: every address recorded in `netguard.Answers`, so the proxy accepts exactly those; a steered claim (`ClaimPolicy.SteerTo`) gets the proxy's address for every allowed name and the mirror.
- The query log: `QueryLog` (`ForClaim`, `Prune`), `QueryRecord` with `Denied` (the allowlist refused it, a person can approve) and `Platform` (the deny list refused it, nothing lifts it).
- The upstream: `Upstream`, `ForwardTo`, `UpstreamAddr` (`UpstreamSystem` reads `resolv.conf`).
- Serving: `serve.go` `ListenAndServe` (UDP or TCP until ctx ends).

## Does not own
- The deny list, address rules and answers store: `internal/netguard`. The policy and its store: `internal/policy`.
- The control port (`PUT /v1/policy`, `GET /v1/queries`): `internal/policy/control` and the process's `main`.

## Rules and limits
- The operator's deny list beats the policy; only owned hosts pass it. The proxy and the resolver must enforce the same list ([`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) "Untrusted input").
- An upstream answer that leads inside the cell is dropped unless it falls in `InternalCIDRs` (`netguard.RefusedDestination`).
- One claim's log holds its last `maxPerClaim` queries: a claim that floods the resolver evicts only its own evidence. A removed claim's answers go at once, its log at `Prune`.
- Data plane: it never imports the control side.

## Tests
- `go test ./internal/resolver` (in-memory upstream, fixed `Now`, local sockets for `ListenAndServe`).

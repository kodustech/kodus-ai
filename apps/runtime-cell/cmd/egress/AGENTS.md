# apps/runtime-cell/cmd/egress

The egress binary: the composition root of the cell's transparent egress proxy ([architecture.md](../../../../docs/runtime/architecture.md), "Egress, injector"). It runs on the cell host beside the agent, takes the claims' redirected 80 and 443, optionally the resolver (`-dns-listen`) and the registry mirror (`-mirror-listen`), and a control port the agent pushes signed policies to.

## Owns
- The data plane as one unit: `plane.go` `newDataPlane` (one `policy.Store` and one answer record shared by `egress.Proxy` and, with `-dns-listen`, `resolver.Server`; the control handler that fills them), `dataPlane.forget` (a deleted claim's connections and answers).
- The injector hand-off address: `plane.go` `checkInjectorAddr` (empty, or `host:port`; anything else stops startup).
- Listeners and servers: `main.go` `start` (TLS and HTTP listeners, DNS, the sweep, the mirror's `/go/`, `/v2/`, `/npm/`, `/pypi/` routes, the control mux with `/v1/queries` and `/metrics/mirror`); `mirrorPublicURL`.
- The mirror's server: `mirror_server.go` `newMirrorServer` (`mirrorConnsPerSource` open connections per machine, `mirrorIdleTimeout` on keep-alive).

## Does not own
- The proxy's decisions (SNI, policy, inspection, upload budget): `internal/egress`. Host rules and the deny list: `internal/netguard`.
- The resolver: `internal/resolver`. The mirror's handlers: `internal/mirror`. Signed policies: `internal/policy`, `internal/policy/control`.

## Rules and limits
- The `cmd/*` rules and the data-plane boundary are in [`apps/runtime-cell/AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) "Package boundaries".
- A standalone resolver keeps its own answer record; only `-dns-listen` lets the proxy accept exactly the addresses the resolver handed out.
- Without `-injector-addr` a policy that names a credentialed host is refused, and says why.
- The mirror serves every claim network from the process every claim depends on: one source past its connection cap is closed at accept, and an idle keep-alive connection after a minute. It sets no `ReadTimeout` or `WriteTimeout`, which would cut a long blob stream.
- `GET /v1/queries` answers only the pinned agent (`-agent-cn`, `-agent-spki`); with no pin, nobody (`control.peer.unpinned` alarm).

## Where new code goes
- A new mirror ecosystem: its handler in `internal/mirror`, its route and upstream flag in `start`.
- A new rule on a connection: `internal/egress` or `internal/netguard`, never here.

## Tests
- `go test ./cmd/egress`: the dev cell's injector trust, the relay of credentialed hosts, refusal without an injector, the operator deny list on both proxy and resolver (`TestTheOperatorListIsDeniedByBothProxyAndResolver`).

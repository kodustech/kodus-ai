# apps/runtime/src/worker/sandbox

The worker side of a code sandbox ([sandbox.md](../../../../../docs/runtime/sandbox.md)): one hub claim of consumer `sandbox` per sandbox, found again from its org and `sandboxId` alone (`claimIdFor` over `sandboxIdempotencyKey`), so any replica serves any call and there is no row of its own. It runs in the runtime-worker behind the signed env-session listener (`worker/env-session`); it only routes and refuses, while the hub records and audits every op.

## Owns
- The sandbox calls: `sandbox.service.ts` `SandboxService.open` (reserve a cell, then `/internal/cells/:id/sandbox`), `op` (`/sandbox-op`), `snapshot` (a session or a round), `template` (a sandbox that prepares, runs its steps, keeps the result under its key and is released), `renew`, `release`.
- Where a sandbox that starts from a snapshot goes: `cellOfSnapshot` prefers the cell that made the template or took the session (`SnapshotPlacements`); a placement that cannot be read is no preference.
- A closed or merged PR's sessions: `pr-sessions.ts` `PullRequestSessions.forget` removes them from every cell that holds one (`/internal/cells/:id/forget-sessions`); a cell out of reach keeps them for the next close or the sweep. Called from `worker/commands/runtime-command-handlers.ts`.

## Does not own
- Shapes, routes and error messages: `libs/runtime/contracts/sandbox.ts`. The product's client: `libs/sandbox/infrastructure/providers/runtime-sandbox.client.ts`.
- The claim, its ops, audit and size admission on the hub: `apps/runtime/src/hub/claims`. Placement rows: `shared/persistence/snapshot-placements.ts`.
- The listener and its signatures: `worker/env-session/env-session-server.ts`.

## Rules and limits
- No sandbox opens while the Runtime is stopped for the org; no connected cell is `no_cell` (503), a capacity refusal a blocked view.
- Every hub call carries `organizationId` and `runId: null` ([apps/runtime/AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) "Tenant scope"); a claim no replica holds is 404.
- A template's own sandbox never prefers the template's cell; a lost placement record only costs a cold boot, and is logged, never failed.
- Waits: acquire 4 min, snapshot 10 min, other calls 30 s; an op waits its own timeout plus a minute.
- A sandbox not given back is taken by its lease.

## Where new code goes
- A new sandbox operation: its schema and route in `libs/runtime/contracts/sandbox.ts` (`SANDBOX_ROUTES`), the `SANDBOX` map in `env-session-server.ts`, a method here over `onClaim`, and the hub route.

## Tests
- `API_NODE_ENV=test npx jest apps/runtime/src/worker/sandbox` (fake hub; one case runs a signed write through the real listener).

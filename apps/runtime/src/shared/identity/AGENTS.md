# apps/runtime/src/shared/identity

Who is on the other end of a TLS connection in the control plane. The hub uses it for its internal routes and its cell certificates, the preview edge for the cells' tunnels. Two small pieces: reading a peer certificate's role and name, and the guard of the hub's internal listener.

## Owns
- `tls-identity.ts` `peerIdentity`: the role (OU) and CN of a peer certificate as Node's TLS layer reports it. Used by `hub/ca/x509.ts`, `hub/gateway/peer-cert.ts` and `edge/edge-app.ts`.
- `internal-auth.guard.ts` `WorkerAuthGuard`: the Nest guard of every `/internal/*` route (`hub/http/internal.controller.ts`). It answers 404 on the public listener, then requires an authorized client certificate with OU=worker and, when `RUNTIME_WORKER_ALLOWED_CNS` is set, a CN on that list.

## Does not own
- The TLS listeners and which CA each trusts: `apps/runtime/src/hub/hub-app.ts`.
- Signing and checking cell certificates, pins and revocations: `apps/runtime/src/hub/ca/`, `hub/enrollment/`.
- Tenant scope on the internal routes (`ClaimRef`): `apps/runtime/src/hub/claims/` ([tenant scope](../../../AGENTS.md#tenant-scope)).

## Rules and limits
- The bearer token is accepted only in test-only plaintext mode (`RUNTIME_TEST_ONLY_PLAINTEXT`), compared in constant time; the production image refuses that mode (`config.ts` `assertSafeForProduction`).
- An internal route on the public listener is a 404, never a 401, so the public side learns nothing about it.
- The guard trusts the handshake: the internal listener must accept only the worker CA, never the cells' CA.

## Where new code goes
- A new peer role (OU): a check next to `role !== 'worker'` in `WorkerAuthGuard`, or a guard of its own here; the certificate that carries it is issued in `hub/ca/`.

## Tests
- `API_NODE_ENV=test npx jest apps/runtime/src/shared/identity`. Both listeners over real mTLS: `apps/runtime/src/hub/go-cell-hub.integration.spec.ts` (opt-in, `RUNTIME_IT_GO_CELL`).

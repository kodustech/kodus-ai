# apps/runtime/src/hub/enrollment

Who a cell is (ADR-054): registration with a single-use token and a CSR, 24 h certificate renewals, managed cells from config, and the per-replica cache of pinned and revoked serials that the TLS handshake checks. It runs in the runtime-hub as `CellHub.identity`; the public controller and the WebSocket gateway call it through `CellHub`.

## Owns
- `cell-identity.service.ts` `CellIdentityService`:
  - `handshakeAllowed`: the synchronous check at `secureConnection` (chains to the CA, not a revoked serial, the pinned serial when known).
  - `authenticateCell`: the only way a cell is authenticated: OU=cell, CN=cellId, the serial pinned on its `runtime_cells` row, not revoked, not expired.
  - `register`: redeems the token, then parses and signs the CSR inside the redemption; `renew`: a valid certificate traded for a new one, the pin moved.
  - `ensureManagedCells`: config cells (`RUNTIME_MANAGED_CELLS`) get a row and a pin, so every cell is revoked through its row.
  - `reloadCertificatePins`, `revokedAmong`: the cache, reloaded by the gateway's 5 s revocation tick.
  - `expiredAmong`: the held cells whose newest certificate expired without a renewal; the same tick ends their open sessions, so a session never outlives its certificate by more than the tick.
- `CELL_CERT_HOURS` (24) and `INVALID_CSR`.

## Does not own
- Signing and CSR parsing: `hub/ca/x509.ts`.
- The registration rate limit: `hub/http/registration-rate-limit.ts`.
- Token issuing and cell revocation: the worker's commands, written in `shared/persistence/pg-command-store.ts`; the rows themselves are in `hub/hub-store.ts`.
- Dropping a revoked or expired cell's live connection: `CellHub.enforceRevocations` and `hub/gateway/cell-connections.ts` `evict`, `closed`.

## Rules and limits
- The token is checked before the CSR is read: nobody without a good token makes the hub parse a CSR or use the CA key.
- A CSR that does not parse throws out of the redemption, which rolls back and keeps the token; the token and the cell row commit together.
- A refused CSR is always the generic `invalid_csr`; the parser's words stay inside.
- A fresh replica refuses every client certificate until its first pin load (`RuntimeHubModule` loads it before the port opens).

## Where new code goes
- A new way a cell proves who it is: a method here called from `CellHub`, with the matching store method in `hub/hub-store.ts` and `hub/testing.ts`.

## Tests
- `API_NODE_ENV=test npx jest apps/runtime/src/hub/enrollment` and `hub/cell-hub.spec.ts` (registration, pins, revocation, managed cells).
- Across a hub restart, with the Go cell: `hub/go-cell-hub.integration.spec.ts`.

# apps/runtime/src/hub/http

The runtime-hub's HTTP routes on its two listeners: the public one for cells (registration, renewal, health) and the internal one for the runtime-worker (`/internal/*`). Thin controllers: they parse the body with zod, map hub errors to status codes and call `CellHub`.

## Owns
- `public-cell.controller.ts` `PublicCellController`: `POST /cells/register` (rate limited per client address), `POST /cells/renew` (over mTLS with the current certificate), `GET /health` (says nothing about connected cells).
- `internal.controller.ts` `InternalController`: every worker route (acquire, sandbox, sandbox-op, progress, probe, browser-explore, exec, reset, snapshot, bake, forget-sessions, pool-target, release, renew, preview-route/unroute, egress-policy, admission reserve/redeem/cancel/retarget-setup, `runs/:runId/events`, `GET /internal/health`) and their body schemas; `mapped` turns `ClaimScopeError` into 404, `CellUnavailableError` and `MemberCleanupPendingError` into 503.
- `admission.schema.ts`: `PhysicalAdmissionRequestSchema`, `PhysicalAdmissionTicketSchema`.
- `registration-rate-limit.ts` `RegistrationRateLimit`: 10 attempts per address per minute, per replica.
- `trace-context.interceptor.ts` `TraceContextInterceptor`: the worker's W3C `traceparent` joins hub spans to the Run's trace.
- `parse-body.ts` `parseBody`: a body that does not parse is a 400.

## Does not own
- Which listener serves which route and the body size limits: `hub/hub-app.ts`.
- The worker's certificate check: `shared/identity/internal-auth.guard.ts` `WorkerAuthGuard`.
- The WebSocket at `/cells`: `hub/cell-gateway.service.ts`.
- The worker's client for these routes: `worker/env/hub-environment-runtime.ts`.

## Rules and limits
- Tenant scope: [apps/runtime/AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md). Every claim route takes `organizationId` (and `runId` for a Run's claim) and builds a `ClaimRef`; a mismatch is 404, never a different error.
- Bodies are strict objects: unknown fields are a 400.
- `cells/register` is the only route without a client certificate; refusals carry a fixed code, never the parser's message.
- `member_cleanup_pending` stays a 503: older workers would release the permit on a success body.

## Where new code goes
- A new worker route: a method on `InternalController` with a strict zod body, the tenant in it, `mapped` around the hub call; a case in `controllers.spec.ts`; the client method in `worker/env/hub-environment-runtime.ts`.

## Tests
- `API_NODE_ENV=test npx jest apps/runtime/src/hub/http` (controllers against a stubbed `CellHub`, no services). Routes on the real listeners: `hub/go-cell-hub.integration.spec.ts`.

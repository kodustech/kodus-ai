# apps/runtime/src/hub

The runtime-hub process: it terminates the cells' mTLS WebSocket and drives claims and steps on them for the runtime-worker, which calls it over the internal listener ([architecture.md](../../../../docs/runtime/architecture.md)). The root files are its composition root, the facade over its parts, the frame router and the store; the parts live in the subfolders. It runs no workflow logic.

## Owns
- Boot and listeners: `main.ts` `bootstrap`; `hub-app.ts` `createRuntimeHubApp` (body limits, 404 for the other listener's routes), `listenRuntimeHub` (the internal listener requires a worker-CA client certificate).
- Wiring: `runtime-hub.module.ts` `RuntimeHubModule` (pools, schema, CA, the stream store, managed cells and pins loaded before the port opens).
- The facade and the frame router: `cell-hub.ts` `CellHub`. `attach` handles one connection's frames in order; `onMessage` decodes every frame, checks `cellId` and direction, demands `hello` first, and routes heartbeats, outcomes, steps, `claim.progress`, `egress.blocked` and unsolicited `blocked`. `acceptHello` (version, owner, isolation, capabilities) and `dropSilentCells` (`CELL_SILENCE_MS`) are here.
- The WebSocket upgrade at `/cells`: `cell-gateway.service.ts` `CellGatewayService` (TLS peer check at `secureConnection`, the revocation and silence ticks).
- What a cell's hello lets it be sent: `cell-capabilities.ts` `capabilitiesOfHello`, `supports`, `grantsSize`, and the blockers for what an older cell cannot do (`sizeUnavailable`, `workspaceUnsupported`, `authoredFilesUnsupported`, `recipeCapabilityBlocker`).
- Persistence: `hub-store.ts` `HubStore` (one repository per aggregate), `PgHubStore`, `CLAIM_REASONS` / `ClaimReason`.
- Shared types and hub metrics: `hub-types.ts` (`ClaimRef`, `ClaimScopeError`, `HubMetrics`, `driverLabel`).
- The in-memory store for specs: `testing.ts` `InMemoryHubStore`.

## Subfolders
- `ca/`: the hub's internal CA, hand-written X.509.
- `claims/`: the claim lifecycle, admission, leases, egress, images, previews and steps.
- `enrollment/`: who a cell is (registration, renewal, pins, revocations).
- `gateway/`: the live socket per cell and its request/ack plumbing.
- `http/`: the public and internal controllers.

## Does not own
- Cell choice and slot reservation for the worker: `shared/persistence/cell-directory.ts`.
- Config and its validation: `shared/config.ts` `loadRuntimeConfig`.
- The protocol schema: `libs/runtime/protocol`.

## Rules and limits
- Layering, claims, tenant scope, logs and metrics: [apps/runtime/AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). The hub never imports `worker/`.
- A frame from a cell on a replaced connection, or before its hello, is never acted on (`isCurrent`, `helloDone`).
- Hellos of one cell run in order (`CellHub.onHello`), so an older session's write never wins last.
- A customer cell's `evictedBuildStates` speak only for its own org and are never dated past a 5 min skew (`syncEvictedBuildStates`).
- `isolation: none` is refused unless `RUNTIME_DEV_ALLOW_INSECURE_CELLS` is set.

## Where new code goes
- A new frame from the cell: the protocol first, then a `case` in `CellHub.onMessage` that hands it to the part that owns it, never logic inline.
- A new optional field a cell may decode: a feature in `cell-capabilities.ts` checked before it is sent.
- A new store method: `HubStore`, `PgHubStore`, `InMemoryHubStore` with the same semantics, a case in `pg-hub-store.integration.spec.ts`.

## Tests
- `API_NODE_ENV=test npx jest apps/runtime/src/hub` (no services).
- `pg-hub-store.integration.spec.ts`: `RUNTIME_IT_DATABASE_URL`, `--runInBand`.
- `go-cell-hub.integration.spec.ts`: the Go cell over mTLS; opt-in with `RUNTIME_IT_GO_CELL=1` (`testing/go-cell.ts` `goCellEnabled`), then `go`, `docker` and `openssl` are required.

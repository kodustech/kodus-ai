---
title: "How to generate code review rules automatically"
description: "Let AI analyze your code review history and suggest custom rules based on your team's actual patterns and standards."
---

Writing code review rules from scratch can be tedious. Kodus can analyze your team's review history and automatically suggest rules based on the patterns it finds.

## How it works

1. Pick the repository in the scope picker at the top of the **Code review** group in the sidebar, open **Kody Rules**, and click **Configuration**
2. Turn on **Generate from past reviews**
3. Kodus scans the last 3 months of closed PRs in your repository
4. It identifies recurring feedback patterns and coding preferences
5. Generates rules tailored to your team, listed on the Kody Rules page with the origin **Kody-generated**

## What happens next

- By default, generated rules are active right away and enforced during code reviews
- To review them before they apply, turn on **Kody knowledge approval** in the same **Configuration** sheet: new rules then wait in the **Pending review** section until you **Approve** or **Discard** them
- Edit, pause, or delete any generated rule like any other Kody Rule

## Continuous learning

After the initial setup, Kodus generates new rules **weekly** as your team's coding style evolves. Check back regularly for new rules.

## Requirements

- At least **3 months** of review history in the repository
- Regular code review activity for better pattern detection
- Repository access with proper permissions

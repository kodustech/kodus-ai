Plans differ by features, not by how many pull requests Kody can review. For prices and commercial terms, see [kodus.io/pricing](https://kodus.io/pricing).

<Note>
  **PR reviews and workspace members are unlimited on every plan.** On a paid plan, developer licenses decide whose pull requests are reviewed. On Free and Teams, reviews run on your own AI key (BYOK). Kody skips a review when it has more than **2,000 reviewable files**, including any files that only a custom agent reviews.
</Note>

## Plans

    Cloud, or self-hosted with a license key. Adds SSO and activity logs.
  </Card>

A self-hosted instance without a license key runs **Community Edition**, which has the same limits as Free. Teams is a cloud plan. To unlock Enterprise on a self-hosted instance, paste the key you received from Kodus under **Subscription → Activate a license key**. When the key expires, the Enterprise features turn off until you paste a renewed one.

Under **Subscription**, **Choose a plan** (**Upgrade** on the Free plan) opens the plan chooser. On a paid plan, **Manage billing** opens Stripe, where you change developer licenses and payment details.

## The free trial

A new Kodus Cloud workspace starts a **14-day trial**. Access to features and the number of reviews Kodus pays for are tracked separately.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="14 days of access" icon="calendar">
    The trial includes Teams features and access to Enterprise SSO and activity logs. Global Rule Sources can import up to five rules total during the trial.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your first 5 PR reviews on us" icon="gift">
    Kodus covers the AI tokens, so you can try Kody with no API key. Trial reviews run on efficient models Kodus provides. Connect your own key to use larger ones.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Subscription** shows how to earn more trial reviews: sign up with a company email, invite teammates, or connect a Git organization with 10 or more developers. **Request review** asks Kodus to look at your trial and extend it.

To keep reviewing past the trial reviews, connect your AI key under [**AI providers**](/en/how_to_use/byok). Reviews then run on your key with no review limit, on any plan, even after the 14 days end. When the trial reviews run out before you connect a key, Kody pauses reviews and, with **Show status feedback** on, posts a comment on the pull request with a link to add one. When the trial ends, choose a plan under **Subscription** to keep the Team features.

On a self-hosted instance without a key, **Request a trial** on **Subscription** asks Kodus for a license key to try.

## Developer licenses

On a paid plan with seats, a developer's pull requests are reviewed when they hold a license. Licenses count developers from your Git organization. Workspace members, the people who configure Kodus, are separate and unlimited.

- Owners assign or release licenses on the **PR licenses** tab of **Subscription**.
- With **Auto-assign licenses** on, a developer's first PR is reviewed without a seat, and a seat is assigned when they open another one, if one is free. See [Auto License Assignment](/en/how_to_use/auto_license_assignment).
- The Free plan and the trial have no per-developer licenses.

## BYOK on every plan

You connect your own provider, and the provider bills you for tokens. On Enterprise, Kodus can instead run the models under your agreement. See [BYOK](/en/how_to_use/byok) for providers and setup, and [Monthly Spend Limit](/en/how_to_use/spend-limit) for spend alerts.

## Payments

Paid plans are billed through Stripe. **Manage billing** on **Subscription** opens the Stripe page for payment methods and invoices, and is available to Owners and Billing Managers. If a payment fails, reviews pause until billing is updated. A canceled or expired subscription also pauses reviews until you subscribe again.

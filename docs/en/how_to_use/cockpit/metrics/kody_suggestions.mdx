## What is the implementation rate

The implementation rate is the share of the suggestions Kody sent that your team implemented. It shows how much of Kody's feedback turns into code changes.

In the Cockpit, it's on the **Kodus Review** tab, as the **Implementation rate** and **Suggestions sent** cards and the charts below them. See [Kodus Review](/en/how_to_use/cockpit/kodus_review).

## How We Calculate It

```
Implementation Rate = (Implemented Suggestions) ÷ (Suggestions Sent) × 100
```

- Only suggestions delivered as PR comments count.
- A suggestion counts as implemented when its status is **Implemented** or **Partially implemented** at the time its PR closes.
- Suggestions on PRs closed in the selected date range count, grouped by the week the PR closed.
- Suggestions the implementation check could not decide are left out of the rate, not counted as ignored.

For example, if Kody sent 20 suggestions and 16 were implemented, your implementation rate is 80%.

## How to Improve

- Configure Kody Rules specific to your team's standards
- Check the **Kody Rules — health** table for rules that are noisy or ignored, then rewrite or scope them
- Look at **Implementation rate by category** for the themes that get ignored most
- Read the 👎 reactions in **Negative feedback** to see where the team disagrees (reactions are recorded as feedback and don't change later reviews on their own; see [Feedback on suggestions](/en/how_to_use/code_review/learning/kody_learning))

## What is PRs Opened vs Closed

This chart puts two weekly counts side by side: how many pull requests were **opened** and how
many were **closed** in the same week. It shows whether the team keeps up with the work it opens,
or whether pull requests pile up faster than they close.

## How We Calculate It

For every week in the selected date range:

- **Opened**: pull requests opened that week, whatever their status today. For a PR opened before
  its repository was connected, and not among the PRs imported at connection, the date is when
  Kodus first recorded it.
- **Closed**: pull requests closed that week, merged or closed without merging.

The two are counted independently: a PR opened in one week and closed three weeks later counts in
the **Opened** bar of the first week and the **Closed** bar of the fourth. That is what makes a
backlog visible.

## How to Read It

- **Closed close to Opened, week after week**: steady state. The team closes what it opens.
- **Opened above Closed for several weeks**: the backlog is growing.
- **Closed well above Opened**: often a batch of stale PRs closed at once. Check before reading it
  as a productivity gain.
- **Both drop at once**: usually a holiday, a freeze or an incident.

<Tip>
  Read this chart next to [PR Cycle Time](/en/how_to_use/cockpit/metrics/lead_time_for_changes).
  A growing gap here with a rising cycle time points to review as the bottleneck.
</Tip>

## Where to Find It

Cockpit → **Productivity** tab, in the detailed charts section. Weeks start on Monday, and you can hide **Opened** or **Closed** by clicking its name under the chart.

<Note>
  This chart can be hidden for your whole organization. If you don't see it, check
  **Organization → Cockpit visibility**. See [Cockpit Configuration](/en/how_to_use/cockpit/configuration).
</Note>

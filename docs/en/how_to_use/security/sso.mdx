## Overview

Single Sign-On (SSO) lets your team sign in to Kodus through your identity provider over SAML 2.0.

Owners configure SAML SSO in **Organization → SSO**.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
    <Step title="Access SSO settings">
        In the sidebar, open **Organization → SSO**
        ([app.kodus.io/organization/sso](https://app.kodus.io/organization/sso)).
        SSO is available on the Enterprise plan (and during a trial); on other plans the page shows a locked preview.
    </Step>

    <Step title="Fill in your IdP configuration">
        Under **SAML SSO Configuration**, turn on **Enable SAML SSO** to show the settings, then fill in your IdP's settings: **IDP Issuer**, **SSO URL** and **X.509 Certificate**. These can be filled in manually or by providing either your IdP's **Metadata URL (optional)** (then **Fetch**) or a metadata XML file (**Upload Metadata XML File**).

        <Info>
            These values can usually be found in your IdP's documentation or in their admin console.
        </Info>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Configure Kodus as a SP in your IdP">
        Configure Kodus as a SP in your IdP by providing our entity ID (**Service Provider Entity ID**, `kodus-orchestrator` by default) and your organization's **Callback URL**, both shown on the SSO settings page.

        Make sure the name ID format is set to email.

        <Warning>
            Ensure you disable signing certificates in your IdP's SP configuration.
        </Warning>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Verify your domains">
        Add your email domains under **Allowed Domains** (public domains such as gmail.com are not allowed) and click **Verify** on each one. In **Verify Enterprise Domain**, enter an email at that domain to receive a verification link (self-hosted instances verify immediately). Each domain must show **Verified** before SSO can be enabled.
    </Step>

    <Step title="Test and enable SSO">
        Click **Test connection** and complete a login at your IdP. SSO can only be saved as enabled after a successful test, so run the test again after you change the IdP settings or domains. After a successful test, click **Save settings** with **Enable SAML SSO** on.
    </Step>

</Steps>

## Optional Configuration

### Mapping Attributes

You can map the following attributes from your IdP to Kodus users, improving the user experience.

-   First Name, mapped to `firstName`
-   Last Name, mapped to `lastName`

## Signing in

On the sign-in page, type your email. If its domain has SSO enabled, Kodus shows **Continue with SSO** instead of asking for a password, and sends you to your IdP.

The first time someone signs in through SSO, Kodus creates their account in your organization with the **Contributor** role, without an email confirmation step. An Owner can change the role afterwards under **Organization → Subscription → Workspace members**; see [Workspace Roles](/en/how_to_use/workspace_roles).

<Note>
**Workspace Members vs. PR Licenses**: These are completely separate and don't affect each other.

- **Workspace Members**: People with Kodus accounts in the workspace. Each has a role that grants access, and every plan allows unlimited members.
- **PR Licenses**: Developers from your Git organization who get Kody reviews (based on your plan)
</Note>

## Understanding PR Licenses

The **PR licenses** tab lists the people who can open pull requests in the repositories Kody reviews: the members of your Git organization, plus recent PR authors, with bots marked. Licenses are assigned to them there, and your plan decides how many seats exist. See [Auto License Assignment](/en/how_to_use/auto_license_assignment).

**Why isn't a developer showing up?** The list comes from your code platform. Click **Refresh members** to reload it. If the person still isn't listed, use **Assign by git ID** to seat them directly. If your code platform doesn't answer, the tab says so and automatic seat cleanup pauses.

**Example:** You can have 1 workspace admin managing settings, while 20 developers get automated reviews. All plans include unlimited PRs; on a paid plan, a license decides which developers get reviewed.

## Overview

Kodus workspaces support **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**, allowing you to manage user permissions and access levels within your organization. Each workspace member has one of four roles, each with specific permissions.

## Available Roles

Each role provides different levels of access and capabilities within the workspace:

### Owner

**Full administrative access** to the workspace and all its resources. Can view, edit, and delete all configurations across all repositories.



### Billing Manager

**Financial and billing management** access. Manages the plan and billing, and can view developer licenses, code review settings, Kody Rules, repositories, plugins, token usage, and activity logs, but cannot edit them. No access to pull requests, Cockpit, or CLI reviews.

### Repository Admin

**Repository management** access. Sees all configurations, pull requests, issues, Cockpit, plugins, and token usage across the whole organization (read-only), and can edit code review configurations, Kody Rules, and issues for the repositories assigned to them. Shown as **Repo Admin** in the app.

### Contributor

**Read-only access** to the whole organization. Contributors can view configurations, Kody Rules, pull requests, and issues across all repositories (no repository assignment needed) but cannot edit anything. Cockpit, token usage, billing, and user management stay admin-only.

## Managing Workspace Members

Workspace members are managed on the **Workspace members** tab of **Organization → Subscription** — there is no separate members page. Only **Owners** can invite members, change roles, or remove members.

### Adding New Members

1. In the sidebar, open **Organization → Subscription** and pick the **Workspace members** tab
2. Click **Invite member**
3. In **Workspace member invitation**, type the member's email and click **Add to invite list** (repeat for more people)
4. Click **Send invites**
5. Invited members join as **Contributor**; change their role afterwards (see below)

### Auto-Join for Workspace Members

Enable auto-join to allow users with matching email domains to join your workspace automatically.

**How to enable:**
1. Go to **Organization → General** ([app.kodus.io/organization/general](https://app.kodus.io/organization/general))
2. Under **Auto Join**, turn on **Enable Auto Join**, enter your domain, and save
3. Currently works with your registered account email domain only
4. Need a different domain? Contact us in the community

**How it works:**
- Users who sign up with your email domain can choose to join your organization
- On Kodus Cloud, they must confirm their email after choosing to join
- All auto-join users enter as **Contributor** role (read-only access to the whole organization)
- As Owner, adjust their role if they need edit rights

### Changing Member Roles

1. Go to **Organization → Subscription → Workspace members**
2. Find the member whose role you want to change
3. Open the select in their **Role** column
4. Select the new role (**Owner**, **Billing Manager**, **Repo Admin**, or **Contributor**)
5. The change takes effect immediately

You can't change your own role. Role changes and member removals notify the organization's Owners.

For a **Repository Admin**, assign the repositories they can edit with the **+** button next to their role, which opens **Assign repositories**.

### Removing Members

1. In **Organization → Subscription → Workspace members**, locate the member to remove
2. Open the **Actions** menu (three dots) in their row
3. Select **Delete**
4. Confirm with **Remove** in the dialog

The same menu has **Approve** for members marked **Needs approval** and **Copy invite link** to share a member's invite link again.

## Security Considerations

1. **Principle of Least Privilege**: Always assign the role with the minimum necessary permissions
2. **Regular Audits**: Periodically review member roles and adjust as needed
3. **Access Reviews**: Conduct quarterly reviews of workspace access and permissions
4. **Immediate Revocation**: Remove access immediately when a member leaves the organization

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't invite new members to the workspace">
    Only Owners can invite members. If you are an Owner and **Invite member** is disabled, check your role under **Organization → Subscription → Workspace members**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A member can't edit code review configurations">
    - Confirm the member is a Repo Admin or an Owner. Billing Managers and Contributors can view settings but not edit them.
    - For a Repo Admin, check that the repository is assigned to them (**Assign repositories** in the **Role** column of **Workspace members**).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Billing information is not visible to a team member">
    **Organization → Subscription** is visible only to Owners and Billing Managers. Repo Admins and Contributors don't see it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A member can't access Cockpit">
    Cockpit is visible to Repo Admins and Owners, not to Contributors or Billing Managers. Cockpit also needs a Teams or Enterprise plan; see [Plans and Licenses](/en/how_to_use/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A developer isn't showing up in PR licenses">
    - Click **Refresh members** on the **PR licenses** tab, and check that the developer belongs to your Git organization or has recently opened PRs in a repository Kody reviews.
    - Use **Assign by git ID** to seat someone who isn't listed.
    - Remember: PR licenses come from your Git provider, not from workspace members.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

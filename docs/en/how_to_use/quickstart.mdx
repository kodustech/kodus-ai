---
title: "Getting Started"
description: "Get Kody reviewing your pull requests: create an account, connect your Git provider, pick repositories, and run a first review."
---

Have access to the Git account and repositories you want to connect. For a self-hosted workspace, use your instance’s URL to sign up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    On Kodus Cloud, open the [sign-up page](https://app.kodus.io/sign-up). For self-hosted Kodus, open the sign-up page on your instance.

    Click **Sign up with Github** or **Sign up with Gitlab**, or sign up with your email. Email sign-up needs a work address: free domains such as Gmail or Outlook are rejected.

    <Frame caption="Sign-up on Kodus Cloud">
      <img src="/images/cloud-sign-up.png" alt="GitHub, GitLab, and corporate email sign-up options" width="440" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    If a workspace has auto join on for your email domain, Kodus shows **We found some organizations!**. Select it and click **Continue** to join it as a Contributor (on Kodus Cloud, you then confirm your email), or click **Create a new organization**.

    On **Let's create a Workspace**, replace the generated **Organization Name** with yours and click **Next**. **Phone** is optional. **Enable Auto Join** starts on with your email domain, unless it is a public one, so anyone with an address on that domain can join; turn it off if you don't want that. See [Auto join](/en/how_to_use/workspace_roles#auto-join-for-workspace-members). The two-question survey after it has **Skip for now**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your Git tool">
    On **Connect your Git tool**, open your provider's tab and connect with the app or a token:

    | Provider | How to connect | Token guide |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | GitHub | **Install Github app** (recommended) or **Connect via token** | [GitHub token](/en/how_to_use/code_review/general_config/github_pat) |
    | GitLab | Sign in with GitLab via OAuth (the **Install Gitlab app** button) or choose **Connect via token** | [GitLab token](/en/how_to_use/code_review/general_config/gitlab_pat) |
    | Bitbucket | **Connect via token** | [Bitbucket token](/en/how_to_use/code_review/general_config/bitbucket_pat) |
    | Azure Repos | **Connect via token** | [Azure DevOps token](/en/how_to_use/code_review/general_config/azure_devops_pat) |
    | Forgejo | **Connect via token** | [Forgejo token](/en/how_to_use/code_review/general_config/forgejo_pat) |

    The GitHub app makes Kody post as its own bot. In every other case, reviews appear under the account that connected: the token owner, or the GitLab user who authorized OAuth.

    A server on your own network (GitHub Enterprise Server, in beta; self-managed GitLab; Bitbucket Data Center; Forgejo) connects with a token and its URL. The token guides show where to enter it.

    <Note>
      Connecting Kodus Cloud to a server on your own network? Allowlist Kodus's IP `52.55.217.197` in your firewall first. On Teams and Enterprise, also turn on **Fixed outbound IP** under **Repositories**, so Kody clones your code from that IP too. On other plans Kody clones from a shared network, which a server that only accepts allowlisted IPs refuses.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select repositories">
    Pick the repositories under **Select repositories**. Under **Who should Kody review?**, choose **PRs opened by me** to try it without affecting the team, or **All PRs in selected repos** (the default). Click **Enable on my PRs** or **Enable for team**. You can add repositories later under **Repositories**.

    If the connected account shows no repositories, the screen says **No repositories found**: reconnect with another account, or a token with more permissions, from **Update connection** or **Try again**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose whether Kody learns from past reviews">
    On **Learn from past reviews?**, turn the switch on to let Kody draft Kody Rules from your team's review comments, and optionally exclude developers under **Whose reviews should Kody learn from?**. It is off by default. Click **Continue** or **Skip for now**. See [Generate rules automatically](/en/how_to_use/code_review/learning/kody_rules_generation).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the minimum severity">
    On **Set up your first review**, choose the lowest severity Kody posts under **Minimum severity level**: **Low/All**, **Medium** (the default), **High** or **Critical**. Click **Continue**. See [Suggestion control](/en/how_to_use/code_review/configs/suggestion_control).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring in your team's rules">
    On **Teach Kody how your team builds software**, Kody lists the Kody Rule files it found in `.kody/rules` and keeps them in sync. The **Use your coding-agent instructions in reviews?** switch lets reviews read files such as `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md`. It is off by default. Click **Continue** or **Skip for now**. See [Repository rules](/en/how_to_use/code_review/configs/repository_rules).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first review">
    On **Kody is ready for your first PR review**, pick a pull request under **Select a PR to review** and click **Review now**, or **Skip for now**. When the selected repositories have no recent pull request, setup ends without this screen. Kody posts the review in your Git tool. After that, it reviews new PRs according to your **Who should Kody review?** choice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After Kody finishes, [see what to look for on the PR](/en/how_to_use/code_review/flow#what-you-get).

## Trial and AI keys

A new Kodus Cloud workspace gets a 14-day trial. Kodus covers the model cost of the first five PR reviews. Connect your own key in **AI providers** to keep reviewing after the included reviews. See [Plans and Licenses](/en/how_to_use/pricing) for trial features and extra reviews.

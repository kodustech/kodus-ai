The middle step is the one worth remembering. The retrieval was delivering the
whole file correctly the entire time — verified directly against the retriever
— while the prompt said "never report a violation whose evidence lies outside
the diff hunks". For a `full-file` rule the evidence IS outside the hunk, so the
instruction forbade exactly the finding the rule exists to make. The remaining
misses are the diff-only control arms, which is the demonstration, not a defect.

NO REGRESSION, BY CONSTRUCTION
──────────────────────────────
Re-classifying the fleet moves rules out of `diff-only`, and a need that fails
to retrieve used to take its rules out of the review entirely. That would let a
single sandbox hiccup silence rules that work today.

`full-file` now DEGRADES instead: the hunk is already part of the file, so
judging without the slice is exactly the pre-feature behaviour, and the claim
checker still guards whatever is asserted. The outward-reaching needs keep
skipping — judging THEM blind is the #1724 bug. Four specs pin this, and
reverting the split fails two of them.
